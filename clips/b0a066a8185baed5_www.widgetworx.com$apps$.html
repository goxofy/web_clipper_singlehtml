My Mac Apps

Everything is made with ♥️ in NYC

I dabble in macOS development as a hobby and specialize in making small utilities, productivity apps, and even simple games. Every program listed here is (reasonably) well-crafted, signed/notarized, and updated (as my free time allows). Best of all, most are completely free or Donation-ware. If you like what you see and want to support my continued work, feel free to buy me a coffee. If you have an idea for a new app or suggestions for new features, contact me.

For details about how your information is handled, check out my privacy policy. Do you want to stay informed about new program launches and updates? Sign up up to stay in the loop! Finally, here's why I don't distribute my apps through the Apple Mac App Store and an overview of my current development philosophy. You can also keep track of updates to my apps by subscribing to my RSS feed.
